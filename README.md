# Buying a House vs Renting and Investing Calculator
This creates a webpage where a user can compare the future net worth when purchasing a house versus renting and investing the principal in the stock market. 

For the home buying choices, the user can set the house value, down payment rate (usually 20%), mortgage interest rate and loan term (typically 30 years), anticipated HOA fees, and expected property tax and maintenance rates (often around 1 \~ 1.5%).

For the renting and investing choices, the user can input their current monthly rent, anticipated annual rate of increases for rent (usually around 3%), and an estimated rate of return on investments in the stock market (over time between 6 \~ 8%).
